## What worked, stated first

The competency is real and should not be re-litigated by this filing. The pass classified **eleven findings** against the three-rung ladder and **never once had to improvise a rung**. Routing worked exactly as written: rung 1 committed with enumerated paths, rung 2 filed as a typed order, rung 3 raised and left open. The defect is narrow and specific: **audit technique for the axis that produced 6 of the 11 findings.**

## The four gaps, as the pass ranked them

**1. The reference-integrity axis has no method — and it is the axis with the findings.**

`janitor.md` states the doctrine ("it binds the pass whether or not a tool exists to mechanize it") and stops. The pass had to invent four probes: root-anchored path extraction, the same widened to whole-canon, provenance expressions checked against their own refs, and relative-link resolution. **A pass running only the recipe the project already ships would have found 1 of the 6 axis-4 findings.** Two findings required probes 3 and 4 specifically; a third required a handle sweep that is not path-checking at all.

The severity is in the counterfactual the pass named itself: *"A different Janitor with the same document would plausibly have run probe 1, reported green, and been **compliant**."* A gate that a conforming implementation can pass while missing five of six real findings is not yet a gate.

**2. The axis's second half is unstated — and this is why divergence A was missed.**

The floor table phrases the axis as *"does every path the canon references resolve, with every absence sanctioned rather than silently tolerated"*. That reads as path-resolution, and it steered the pass into building resolution probes exclusively.

But the axis also covers **cross-document consistency of the declaration's own restatements**: two documents disagreeing about which absences are sanctioned, *while every path in both resolves perfectly*. There is no path to fail. No resolution probe can ever see it — which is precisely the shape of seeded divergence A, and precisely why it dropped out of the cold record.

The phase doc anticipated this exactly (*"caught by reading"*; *"the script is the durable prevention, not the detector"*). **`janitor.md` does not say it, and `janitor.md` is what the Janitor reads.** The pass's own assessment, which I endorse: this is the highest-value fix available — one sentence stating that reference integrity has two halves, *do referenced paths resolve* and *do the documents that declare what may be absent agree with each other*, would have caught the mission's own seeded subject cold.

**3. Two sections give opposite instructions for the same finding.**

§ The five audit axes: the `work/todos/` slice *"delegates wholesale to `/triage`; the Janitor never re-audits it"*, naming archive routing explicitly. § The remedy ladder: *"archiving closed orders"* is named first among rung-1 auto-fixes.

Finding A5-1 was exactly that case — a closed order left in the inbox. The pass applied the fix, reading § axes as governing *who audits* and § ladder as governing *what a finding's remedy is*. That reading is defensible and it is **the pass's own, not the document's**. A Janitor reading it the other way leaves the order in place and is equally compliant.

This is the gap that bears on **milestone acceptance criterion 7**, which requires that a reader of the docs can "run a pass and know which rung each finding belongs to." Here two sections answer differently.

**4. The automatic-mode `ESCALATE` clause contradicts the advisory order-close hat.**

§ The remedy ladder: *"In automatic mode, an unresolved rung-3 fork stops with an `ESCALATE` Acceptance Verdict."* § The hat pattern: at work-order close the hat is *"light checklist, advisory"* — and `manager/CLAUDE.md` § Closing Out says it *"never blocks this close"*.

Phase 4a was a Manager in automatic mode that found **three** genuine rung-3 forks. Read literally, `janitor.md` required it to terminate with `ESCALATE`; the boundary it was standing at said the pass never blocks. **The campaign avoided the collision only because confirmed decision D-D had pre-ruled raised-and-open as valid** — i.e. an operator decision, made for other reasons, is the only thing that kept the product self-consistent in its first live run.

The clause is plainly written for the *blocking milestone-close* pass, where halting is the design. It needs its boundary qualified rather than applying to "automatic mode" flatly.

**5. Minor.** § Output convention says *where* the pass records itself, never *what shape* the record takes. The five-axes-with-rung-and-evidence structure came from the work order, not the agent doc. A bare `/clean` would have to invent it, and two `/clean` runs would produce two shapes.
