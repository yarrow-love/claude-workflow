### The discriminant, as doctrine must treat it

> **`run_in_background: false` is honored iff the dispatched agent's turn ends with no live background children of its own. If its turn ends while a background child is still live, the harness converts the dispatch to background at that instant.**

Reactive, at the **callee's** turn-end (42 ms / 48 ms after its turn-ending text, seconds after the dispatch). Fits 10/10 dispatches plus battery 1's call site. **A caller cannot secure synchronous collection with this parameter; the callee revokes it unilaterally.** Batch size and the `model` override were both falsified as candidates.

### The three return paths

| Path | Where the result lands |
| --- | --- |
| Dispatched background child, dispatcher's turn ends while it is live | into the **dispatcher's** own loop |
| SendMessage continuation of a completed agent | **to the root** — stopped *or* live dispatcher alike |
| Dispatched background child, dispatcher outlives it | **no observable delivery anywhere** (Q2-c) |

### 2. The role-scoped allowance — authored by the mission architect

Layered on the unconditional invariant. **No session introspects its depth; the role ladder is the depth oracle.**

> **Dispatch collection, by role.**
> — A **Manager** or **specialist**, in any seat — including a Manager the operator runs directly — dispatches every Agent-tool child with `run_in_background: false` and collects it **in-turn**; it never ends a turn while a child it launched is live. Fan-out is parallel dispatches in a single message, collected together in that turn.
> — The **Executor** — the campaign's root by construction — may dispatch with `run_in_background: true` and end its turn while children run; it collects at its **turn boundaries** via harness notifications, reads progress from the work-doc ledger, and steers mid-flight by SendMessage.
> — **No role collects a background result by in-turn waiting.** There is no in-turn delivery path for a background result at any tier; a session that polls for one starves it. Background results cross turn boundaries only.
> — **Why the split is safe, stated for the reader**: a child that collects in-turn is exactly a child whose own dispatcher's synchronous collection the platform honors — the rule enforces itself downward. The root's allowance exists because the root's collector is the harness loop itself, the one delivery path stable across known routings.

**The third clause is load-bearing and binds the Executor too.** Q2-c's starvation generalizes: the root's notifications also arrive at turn boundaries, so *"the root may await"* always means **end the turn and receive** — never *poll inside one*.
