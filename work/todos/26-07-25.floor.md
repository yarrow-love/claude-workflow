# Phase 1 — floor: the declared-structure doctrine into canon

## Feature Request

> executor@play/9362ff9f-c270-4704-97cc-14d527ba9f12

Seeded from [`work/milestones/1.janitor/1.floor.md`](../milestones/1.janitor/1.floor.md) — **the phase doc stays the spec and the record; this order is the build surface.** Read it first, then this document's Reconnaissance and Confirmed decisions, which harden it.

Make declaredness + reconciliation a stated invariant of the workflow, and give every consuming project a schema to declare its structure against. The floor must be statable without a single project-shaped fact entering `.claude/` — that is the phase's hardest constraint and its acceptance test.

Three surfaces:

1. **The doctrine — `work/README.md`.** A new section stating the invariant, block-quoted verbatim: **Every consuming project declares its structure; the system owns reconciling actual against declared.** It names the five audit axes, states why four of the five are not universally *specifiable* and that the meta-pattern inverted is the true maximal-minimum universal, and names `work/PROJECT.md § Structure` as the declaration's home.
2. **The schema — `.claude/BASELINE.md` bootstrap step 3.** Specify the § Structure section rather than merely exemplifying it: five subsections, each with a template default a project amends. Extend § Expectations so the keystone-docs expectation names § Structure.
3. **The exemplar — `work/PROJECT.md § Structure`.** This repo's own declaration, all five subsections filled, its sanctioned-absences subsection transcribed from the seven-entry gate recipe in § All roles item 1.

The role-ladder row (Janitor) and the command-family row (`/clean`) are **phase 2's** — they describe artifacts that do not exist yet. Do not add them.

**The axes have zero substantive drift** across the proposal, charter, milestone, and phase doc. Use the proposal's noun forms: **root discipline · product/non-product separation · tracked-tree cleanliness · whole-canon reference integrity · work-tracking fidelity**. Two content gaps to author, not to look up:

The in-tree evidence for what it means: `.gitignore`'s groups (secrets, machine-local Claude state, `tmp/`, `node_modules/`) and BASELINE § Expectations' "Git hygiene". **Genericity hazard nobody wrote down.** The gate's extraction regex scans all of `.claude/**/*.md` for `(\.claude/bin|work|docs|src|tmp)/…`, so **any illustrative path written into BASELINE prose becomes a tree assertion**. `src/lib/`, `docs/api/`, `work/notes/x.md` would all fail acceptance 8. Use `<>` placeholders — `src/<subsystem>/`, `work/missions/<v>/` — which the recipe's `grep -vE '<|>'` filter drops.

**Baseline to preserve.** Gate item 1 is green: 30 references, 4 unresolved, all 4 sanctioned. The genericity grep has **exactly one** pre-existing hit — `.claude/BASELINE.md:3`, the canonical-home git URL. A second hit is a failure.

**Do not touch, though you will work next door to them**: anything footgun-shaped (`work/footguns/README.md` and the seven `.claude/` policy docs claiming a root-`CLAUDE.md` footgun index) — that is seeded divergence B, phase 4's subject; the Janitor/`/clean` canon rows (phase 2's); `work/milestones/README.md` § Conventions and `plan-milestone.md` (phase 3's); and `work/PROJECT.md` gate item 1's grep, which must not be made to invoke a script that does not exist until phase 4.

## Confirmed decisions

> executor@play/9362ff9f-c270-4704-97cc-14d527ba9f12

Locked at the campaign's Reconnaissance checkpoint with the operator. Binding — if one proves wrong, raise it; do not reinterpret it.

1. **The doctrine section is `## The declared-structure floor`, inserted before `## The follow-through rule`**, opening with `**Stated here once; referenced everywhere else.**` and then the block quote verbatim, character-for-character.
2. **The five axes are a three-column table**: `| Axis | What the pass asks | What answers it |`. Column 3 names the § Structure subsection that answers it (or, for work-tracking fidelity, the template canon). Phase 2 cites column 1 verbatim and does not restate columns 2–3; phase 4's pass reports against these five names.
3. **Reconcile all three copies of the supplement-question list in this phase** — `.claude/BASELINE.md` § Expectations (acceptance 4), `.claude/commands/initialize.md`, and `work/README.md` § Layout's `PROJECT.md` row — and say so in the Summary, so phase 3 knows its conditional `/initialize` item is discharged.
4. **§ Structure's headings and grammar are a parser contract, pinned in BASELINE step 3.** Phase 4's `reference-gate` reads the sanctioned-absence list out of this section. Pin in step 3, as spec: the exact heading strings, and the rule that **the first inline-code span on each `- ` bullet is the path**, prose after it ignored. Without this the contract would live in phase 4's script instead of the spec, and a consuming repo authoring § Structure from BASELINE would have no way to know what the tool looks for. Recommended headings — substitute only with reason, since phases 2 and 4 cite them: `## Structure`, `### Root-file allowlist`, `### Product / non-product boundary`, `### Package roots`, `### Archive conventions`, `### Sanctioned absences`.
5. **Step 3's sanctioned-absences default is a cross-reference, never an enumeration.** State it as *"the post-scaffold paths named in step 2; a project amends by adding what its own commands lazily create."* Writing a second list into step 3 would leave BASELINE self-contradicting at two line numbers — indistinguishable from a phase-1 mistake when phase 4 audits it, and a manufactured divergence phase 4 was not seeded to find. Keep exactly two lists in the repo: step 2's, and the declaration's.
6. **`## Structure` goes immediately after `## All roles` in `work/PROJECT.md`**, before `## Front-end surface` — cross-cutting sections lead in that document, and it lands adjacent to the gate recipe whose list it transcribes. Untagged, like `## All roles`.
7. **The declaration gains a declared exclusions category.** Beyond the phase doc's five subsections, § Structure carries the containers excluded from whole-canon reference gating — `work/proposals/` and `work/notes/`, because root `CLAUDE.md` forbids rewriting historical documents and gating them would manufacture permanently-unfixable failures. Declared here, so phase 4's engine reads it from the seam rather than hardcoding a skip-list. Also declare the **scope roots** the sweep walks, for the same reason: hardcoding `.claude|work|docs|src|tmp` in a script would make "zero project facts" only half-true.
8. **Root-file allowlist for the exemplar lists tracked entries plus the ignored-by-policy ones, marked as such** (`tmp/`, `.archive/`) — they are convention-created and would otherwise read as undeclared drift to phase 4's own audit.
