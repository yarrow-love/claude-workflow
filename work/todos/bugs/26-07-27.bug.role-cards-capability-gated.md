# Five role-card sites gate graph usage on capability or relevance, never on the seam declaration

> manager@play/a377b823-57a2-4f74-b03b-049170e6cef3

**Symptom.** Five sites gate their graph guidance on something other than the seam declaration (`work/PROJECT.md` § Codebase graph). Four open on per-machine **capability** — "When the codebase-memory MCP is available…": `.claude/agents/researcher/CLAUDE.md:26`, `.claude/agents/investigator.md:26`, `.claude/agents/planner.md:26`, `.claude/agents/executor/workflows/execute-milestone.md:9`. The fifth gates on **relevance** — "when a dispatch relies on graph results" (`.claude/agents/manager/CLAUDE.md:95`). On a machine where the MCP is configured user-scope but the repo declares `None`, each says "use the graph" — or, at the manager site, "verify the graph" — with nothing to use.

**Why this is redundancy, not contradiction.** The evidence-passing rule already degrades the case gracefully — a brief carrying no verification means grep fallback (`.claude/protocols/codebase-graph.md` § Evidence-passing dispatch) — so the wrong gate costs a moment of misdirection, never wrong behavior. That is why the M2 hardening ruled it out of the doctrine phase rather than amending five role cards mid-campaign; `execute-milestone.md` was additionally owned by the running campaign itself (dispatched-file-ownership hazard, `.claude/ADR.md` § Standing hazards).

**The fix, when taken.** Align the gate with the matched pair's semantics — applicability is per-repo (the seam declaration), capability per-machine — the clause boundary `.claude/BASELINE.md` § Expectations and `.claude/commands/initialize.md` step 2 now draw. One sentence per card; no doctrine change. Line numbers current as of filing.
