> Status: pending — **stub**; harden via `/plan work/milestones/1.janitor`. The mission's single milestone (operator amendment at commission): the three phases below carry what a three-milestone shape would have.

## Objective

Enact the adopted repo-hygiene proposal end to end: canonize the declared-structure floor, build the Janitor competency and its gates, and prove the system on this mission's own tree — closing only through the very gate it introduces (the first blocking milestone-close pass is this milestone's own).

## Scope (gist)

- **Floor into canon** (charter D2): `work/PROJECT.md § Structure` defaults into BASELINE's bootstrap guidance; the declaredness + reconciliation doctrine and the five audit axes into `work/README.md`; this repo's own § Structure declaration authored as the exemplar.

## Phases (gist)

Roughly: (1) **floor** — doctrine + declarations, (2) **build** — janitor.md, clean.md, gate wiring across the role docs, (3) **prove** — dogfood `/clean`, enactment filing, fold preparation. `/plan` decides the exact split, per-phase acceptance, and which phase carries the genericity audit.

## Acceptance (gist)

The mission's success criteria 1–5, verified at this milestone's close — through the new blocking pass itself: this milestone cannot close until the gate it built says so, or escalates.
