**The discriminant, named — and it is a deterministic rule, not nondeterminism.**

> **`run_in_background: false` is honored iff the dispatched agent's turn ends with no live background children of its own. If its turn ends while a background child is still live, the harness converts the dispatch to background at that instant.**

The decision is **reactive**, made at the *callee's* turn-end rather than at dispatch: the root's `Async agent launched successfully.` returns 42 ms and 48 ms after the dispatcher's turn-ending text, seconds after the dispatch itself. It fits **10 of 10** dispatches plus battery 1's call site. Both seeded candidates were falsified — batch size and the `model` override — and the escalation verdict `nondeterministic within identical parameters` was **deliberately withheld**, correctly: the bar requires transcript-verified identical parameters, and the divergent trials differed in child workload. **A caller cannot secure synchronous collection through this parameter; the callee's own lifecycle decides.**

**Three asynchronous return paths, mapped — "all collapse to the root" was wrong in both directions.**

| Path | Where the result lands |
| --- | --- |
| Dispatched background child, dispatcher's turn ends while it is live | **Into the dispatcher's own loop** (Q2-a, Q2-b, Q6; root `enqueue`→`remove` 53–75 ms apart, `remove` timestamped identically to delivery) |
| SendMessage continuation of a completed agent | **To the root** — whether the dispatcher is stopped (Q4-a, from a *verified* clean stop) or **live** (Q4-b, polling ~30 s). Version-stable: instance 8 replicates it on 2.1.218 |
| Dispatched background child, dispatcher outlives it | **No observable delivery anywhere** (Q2-c) — enqueued at the root, removed 10.9 s later, absent from every transcript |

**Two findings that change doctrine's shape.** The **asymmetry claim dies in its operative form**: a depth-2 child reached its **parent** by raw `agentId`, delivered into the parent's own loop, and reached `main` as well — by *name* it failed 2/2 live and 4/4 in the corpus with character-identical error text. The surviving constraint is **discoverability, not addressability**: an agent cannot determine its own id, so the parent-reaching address must be injected from above. And **Q2-c falsified the polling recovery**: a live dispatcher that polls receives nothing, so "collect your background child before ending your turn" describes an action with no observed mechanism.
