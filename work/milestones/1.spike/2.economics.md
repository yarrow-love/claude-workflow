# Phase 2 — economics: the cost matrix, the heartbeat, and the gate

## Objective

Measure the same consult question four ways plus a heartbeat, from the channel's own emitted `usage` events, and convert the numbers into the three verdicts the mission's remaining roadmap depends on: **does a channel turn hit the warm cache**, **does a heartbeat hold warmth cheaply**, and **is the warm-fork corollary true**. Append the raw record to the evidence note (D6) and put the recommendation in front of the operator, whose reading is the mission's go/no-go gate.

Rated `medium`: the matrix is prescribed by the charter and phase 1 leaves the instrument built, so the design content here is the *ordering discipline* (cold cells are destroyed by any prior touch) and the *honest extrapolation* from a forced 5-minute TTL to the production 1-hour one — not novelty.

## Scope

### The TTL regime, and its declared cost

All cells run under **`FORCE_PROMPT_CACHING_5M=1`** (operator ruling 3, `MILESTONE.md` § Settled at planning) so an expiry cell costs six idle minutes instead of sixty-one.

**Validity check, first and blocking.** The forcing variable is documented but unverified here. Before any matrix cell, confirm the regime actually took effect: warm the vehicle prefix, idle **>6 minutes**, then issue a turn and confirm the tokens land in `cache_creation_input_tokens` rather than `cache_read_input_tokens`. If they land in `cache_read`, the 5-minute forcing did **not** take effect and the expiry-sensitive cells — (d) and the heartbeat-efficacy check — fall back to a **native 1-hour idle wait**, with the longer run recorded as such. Do not report 5-minute numbers that the check did not license.

**The extrapolation is a first-class deliverable, not a footnote.** Claude Code's production default is the 1-hour TTL. Every cadence figure this phase produces for M2's warmth policy is therefore *reasoned* from 5-minute measurements, and the evidence note must say which numbers are measured and which are inferred, in the same breath as the number itself.

### Measuring forks

`.claude/bin/consult` captures **no** token data — it treats the child's stdout as an opaque string and never sets `--output-format`. The fork cells are therefore measured by the harness directly, spawning `claude -p --resume <vehicle> --fork-session --output-format stream-json --verbose …` with `consult`'s five read-only layers reproduced verbatim (phase 1 § The spawn construction). Mirroring `consult`'s flag construction exactly is what makes the measurement a measurement *of the production consult path* rather than of a lookalike.

That `consult` cannot report its own cost is itself a finding worth recording — M3 inherits it when the consult path learns the channel rung.

### The matrix

Same question, same vehicle transcript, every cell. Capture per cell: all four `usage` token fields, wall-clock to first output token (the charter names prefill latency as the other half of the regression), and a timestamp.

| Cell | What | Precondition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| a | **Cold fork** — resume-fork consult against a cold prefix | Vehicle untouched for > TTL. Phase 1's own runs warm it, so the phase opens with an enforced idle gap |
| b1 | **Warm fork after a fork** — the corollary in its pure form | Issued immediately after cell (a), same byte-exact prefix |
| b2 | **Warm fork after a channel turn** — the operationally relevant form | Issued immediately after a channel turn. Note the nuance in the analysis: a channel is itself a fork whose prefix *diverges* as turns accrete, so only the shared vehicle-prefix portion can hit — report which portion did |
| c | **Channel turn within TTL** | Second turn of a live channel, sent right after the first |
| d | **Channel turn after TTL expiry** | Same channel, after an idle gap > TTL with no heartbeat |
| e | **Heartbeat turn** — its own cost | A trivial prompt on a warm channel |

**Ordering is load-bearing.** Warmth is destructive of the cold measurement and cannot be undone except by waiting. Cell (a) must be first, after an enforced idle gap; the run script enforces the gaps rather than trusting the operator's timing, and the log's timestamps must make the enforcement auditable.

**Heartbeat efficacy is a separate measurement from heartbeat cost.** Cell (e) prices a beat; it does not show a beat *works*. Add the efficacy check: idle just under the TTL, send a heartbeat, idle just under the TTL again, then send a real turn — and confirm it reads cache rather than writing it. D2 ("warmth is a maintained property") rests on this check, not on cell (e).

### The analysis

- **Ratios per cell** — `cache_read` versus `cache_creation` as a share of the prefix, plus the wall-clock spread between warm and cold. Ratios are the verdict; absolute magnitude at this vehicle's scale is presentational (ruling 2), and M4 supplies real-campaign magnitude.
- **The warm-fork corollary, ruled explicitly** — confirmed or refuted, with the numbers, for both b1 and b2. A confirmation means the fork mechanism was never intrinsically expensive and parallel consults stay cheap whenever the channel is warm; that materially shapes M2's isolation story and M3's degrade path.
- **The heartbeat break-even** — expressed as *how many beats an idle window can absorb before beating costs more than one cold write*, from measured (e) and (a). State the 1-hour-TTL cadence M2 should adopt, marked as inferred.
- **The collection-point observation** — `work/notes/26-07-25.child-collection-evidence.md` predicts a persistent superior is a collection point a turn ending cannot abandon. The charter asks whether it holds; record what the channel's behaviour across turns actually showed, even if the answer is "not exercised by this spike."

## Acceptance

Headlessly verifiable unless marked.

1. **The TTL validity check ran and its outcome is recorded** — forced-5m effective, or the fallback to a native 1-hour wait taken. No matrix cell is reported without it.
2. **All six measurements captured** — cells a, b1, b2, c, d, e — each with the four `usage` token fields, wall-clock to first token, and a timestamp. Raw stream logs for every cell retained under `tmp/spike/`.
3. **Ordering is auditable** — the log's timestamps show cell (a) preceded every warming touch and show each required idle gap actually elapsed.
4. **The heartbeat efficacy check ran** — beat-then-idle-then-real-turn, with the real turn's cache disposition recorded. Reported separately from cell (e)'s cost.
5. **The warm-fork corollary is ruled** — confirmed or refuted in one sentence, backed by b1's and b2's numbers, with b2's shared-prefix-versus-divergent-tail behaviour stated.
6. **The break-even is a number with its caveat attached** — beats-per-idle-window from measured (a) and (e), plus the recommended 1-hour-TTL cadence explicitly marked *inferred, not measured*.
7. **The evidence note carries the raw record** — a dated section in `work/notes/26-07-28.consult-token-cost-evidence.md` with the full per-cell table (numbers, not prose summary), the three verdicts, and the extrapolation declaration; section-stamped per `work/README.md` § The callback grammar.
8. **A recommendation is written, and it is only a recommendation** — the phase states go or no-go with its reasoning; it does not rule.
9. **The gate is ruled — operator-gated.** The operator reads the numbers and records go / no-go in `MILESTONE.md` § Acceptance. A no-go closes the mission unaccomplished (charter failure criterion 1) and the Executor stops rather than proceeding to M2.
10. **Quality gate green** — `bun .claude/bin/reference-gate check`, the tooling smoke test, and `/initialize` per `work/PROJECT.md` § All roles. Note that `work/notes/` is a gate exclusion and `work/milestones/` is a planning-artifact root (warns, never fails), so this phase should introduce no new failures; if it does, they are real.
11. **No product changed.** `git status` shows no modification under `.claude/`.

## Depends on

- **Phase 1 closed, and its gate passed.** If phase 1 refuted live transcript append, the event wire, or the read-only boundary, this phase does not run — the milestone escalates the refutation instead. Economics are irrelevant to a channel that cannot be observed or bounded.
- **Phase 1's harness works and its wire format is written down.** This phase extends that harness; it does not rebuild it.
- **Phase 1 criterion 5's answer about where `usage` rides.** If per-turn `usage` is not emitted inside a multi-turn streaming process and only a terminal event carries it, the channel cells (c, d, e) must be restructured — most likely one process per measured turn, resumed rather than held open, which weakens cell (c)'s fidelity. Record the restructuring and its fidelity cost if it proves necessary; do not silently substitute.
- **The vehicle transcript from phase 1 is still present** and is the same one, so every cell shares a byte-exact prefix. A different vehicle between phases invalidates the matrix.
