# Phase 2 — economics: the cost matrix, the heartbeat, and the gate

## Objective

Measure the same consult question four ways plus a heartbeat, from the channel's own emitted `usage` events, and convert the numbers into the three verdicts the mission's remaining roadmap depends on: **does a channel turn hit the warm cache**, **does a heartbeat hold warmth cheaply**, and **is the warm-fork corollary true**. Append the raw record to the evidence note (D6) and put the recommendation in front of the operator, whose reading is the mission's go/no-go gate.

Rated `medium`: the matrix is prescribed by the charter and phase 1 leaves the instrument built, so the design content here is the *ordering discipline* (cold cells are destroyed by any prior touch) and the *honest extrapolation* from a forced 5-minute TTL to the production 1-hour one — not novelty.

## Scope

### The TTL regime, and its declared cost

All cells run under **`FORCE_PROMPT_CACHING_5M=1`** (operator ruling 3, `MILESTONE.md` § Settled at planning) so an expiry cell costs six idle minutes instead of sixty-one.

**The regime check is a field read, not a standalone probe** (redesigned at the Reconnaissance gate on the mission architect's ruling, superseding the warm-idle-reread check this doc originally specified). That check was defective twice over: its own confirmatory turn touched the vehicle prefix — destroying the cold state cell (a) requires untouched, the baseline every other ratio is computed against — and it was an indirect proxy for something one field answers directly.

Cache-write tokens ride a **nested `usage.cache_creation` object** carrying `ephemeral_5m_input_tokens` and `ephemeral_1h_input_tokens`, which reports *which TTL a write actually claimed*, on the write itself, with no idle wait. Therefore:

- **Read it on cell (a)'s own first write.** The cold-fork baseline turn doubles as the regime check — zero extra turns, nothing touched early.
- **Assert it on every write cell**, not just the first, so the regime is verified across the whole run and a mid-run anomaly is caught at the cell where it happened.
- **Cite cell (d) as the behavioural cross-check.** The field read licenses the write-*tagging* claim; it does not by itself prove a 5m-tagged entry expires on schedule. Cell (d) already proves exactly that by idling past the TTL and landing in `cache_creation` rather than `cache_read`. Naming it as the cross-check gives independent behavioural confirmation for free, where the design needs it anyway.
- **Do not insert a second idle gap.** It buys nothing the matrix does not already produce.

**Added cell — the ambient TTL** (new at the gate). Charter determination D1 asserts "Claude Code sessions use the 1-hour TTL" as *charter-verified*. Reconnaissance reports the default is **auth-method dependent** — subscription/OAuth login defaults to 5 minutes, API-key auth to 1 hour — and this environment shows no `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`. If that holds, D1's line was environment-contingent and belonged on the spike-owned list. Settle it with **one unforced turn** (no `FORCE_PROMPT_CACHING_5M` in its environment) reading the same nested breakdown, and record the auth mode alongside it. It is the cheapest possible conversion of a charter-versus-recon dispute into measured fact, which is precisely the spike's job.

**What the ambient finding changes, and what it doesn't.** If ambient is 5 minutes, ruling 3's extrapolation caveat largely *dissolves* — the forced regime **is** production, and the forcing flag becomes belt-and-suspenders that makes the regime explicit rather than incidental. The mission's premise survives: the architect's arithmetic puts the hold-warm break-even at **G ≈ 57 minutes** (holding warm across a quiet gap of G costs ≈ (G/5) × 0.1× of the prefix; letting it lapse costs the next consult ≈ 1.15× extra), so holding warm wins for any quiet gap under about an hour, campaign traffic refreshes the TTL for free, and the latency half of the win is TTL-independent. What it *does* change is M2's inheritance: a gap-aware warmth policy (hold warm inside the break-even horizon, lapse deliberately beyond it) rather than a trivial hourly heartbeat — plus a design input, whether the TTL regime is itself a controllable lever.

**Amending D1 is the operator's, not this phase's.** Determinations are operator-signed; the phase measures and recommends. Carry the recommendation — amend D1's TTL sentence to state the auth-dependence as measured, and move the production-TTL question onto the retired spike-owned list — to the gate, with the approving session's handle recorded if accepted.

**Whichever way it lands, say which numbers are measured and which are reasoned**, in the same breath as the number itself.

### Measuring forks

`.claude/bin/consult` captures **no** token data — it treats the child's stdout as an opaque string and never sets `--output-format`. The fork cells are therefore measured by the harness directly, spawning `claude -p --resume <vehicle> --fork-session --output-format stream-json --verbose …` with `consult`'s five read-only layers reproduced verbatim (phase 1 § The spawn construction). Mirroring `consult`'s flag construction exactly is what makes the measurement a measurement *of the production consult path* rather than of a lookalike.

That `consult` cannot report its own cost is itself a finding worth recording — M3 inherits it when the consult path learns the channel rung.

### The matrix

**One shared consult question** for cells a, b1, b2, c and d; cell (e) is a deliberately trivial prompt, because pricing a heartbeat is the point of it. (This resolves a contradiction in the doc as first written, which claimed one question for all six.) Same vehicle transcript throughout — a different vehicle between cells or between phases invalidates the matrix.

Capture per cell: all four flat `usage` token fields **plus the nested `cache_creation` breakdown**, wall-clock to first output token (the charter names prefill latency as the other half of the regression), and a timestamp.

**Wall-clock repeats — 3× on the cold/warm fork pair, 1× elsewhere** (operator ruling at the gate). Token ratios are mechanically deterministic given a fixed prefix and a confirmed regime, so one sample settles every cache verdict. Latency is not: this machine runs concurrent sessions and the harness tees a multi-megabyte log while measuring. Repeat the two cells the latency claim actually rests on — cold fork and warm fork — and report median plus spread; single-sample latency elsewhere carries a noise caveat.

**Preconditions the harness owns, not the operator.** The enforced idle gaps exceed a single tool call's ceiling, so they must be the harness's **own internal async sleep** — never a shell `sleep` a dispatching agent waits on, which ends the turn with the work ownerless. Record the vehicle's mtime immediately before each cold cell as cheap contamination evidence.

| Cell | What | Precondition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| a | **Cold fork** — resume-fork consult against a cold prefix | Vehicle untouched for > TTL. Phase 1's own runs warm it, so the phase opens with an enforced idle gap |
| b1 | **Warm fork after a fork** — the corollary in its pure form | Issued immediately after cell (a), same byte-exact prefix |
| b2 | **Warm fork after a channel turn** — the operationally relevant form | Issued immediately after a channel turn. Note the nuance in the analysis: a channel is itself a fork whose prefix *diverges* as turns accrete, so only the shared vehicle-prefix portion can hit — report which portion did |
| c | **Channel turn within TTL** | Second turn of a live channel, sent right after the first |
| d | **Channel turn after TTL expiry** | Same channel, after an idle gap > TTL with no heartbeat |
| e | **Heartbeat turn** — its own cost | A trivial prompt on a warm channel |

**The channel instance is this phase's own.** Phase 1's held-open process does not survive a work-order boundary; phase 2 opens a **fresh** held-open channel — a new fork of the same vehicle — and that channel-open event is itself measured and labelled, not left as a silent unmeasured seventh event. Its first turn is mechanistically a warm fork, which is what makes it the natural home for cell b2.

**Ordering is load-bearing.** Warmth is destructive of the cold measurement and cannot be undone except by waiting. Cell (a) must be first, after an enforced idle gap; the run script enforces the gaps rather than trusting the operator's timing, and the log's timestamps must make the enforcement auditable.

**Heartbeat efficacy is a separate measurement from heartbeat cost.** Cell (e) prices a beat; it does not show a beat *works*. Add the efficacy check: idle just under the TTL, send a heartbeat, idle just under the TTL again, then send a real turn — and confirm it reads cache rather than writing it. D2 ("warmth is a maintained property") rests on this check, not on cell (e).

### The analysis

- **Ratios per cell** — `cache_read` versus `cache_creation` as a share of the prefix, plus the wall-clock spread between warm and cold. Ratios are the verdict; absolute magnitude at this vehicle's scale is presentational (ruling 2), and M4 supplies real-campaign magnitude.
- **The warm-fork corollary, ruled explicitly** — confirmed or refuted, with the numbers, for both b1 and b2. A confirmation means the fork mechanism was never intrinsically expensive and parallel consults stay cheap whenever the channel is warm; that materially shapes M2's isolation story and M3's degrade path.
- **b2's shared-prefix figure is an inference and must be labelled one.** `usage` reports aggregate totals; it does not attribute which bytes of a hit came from which historical write, so "how much of the hit was the shared vehicle portion" can only be inferred against an independently-known baseline (cell a's or b1's write total). Sharper still: cache hits require **breakpoint alignment**, not merely prefix overlap — if the channel's breakpoint sits after its own first turn, a vehicle-only fork may get **zero** partial hit rather than a partial one. That outcome is a legitimate finding, not a failed measurement; report it as such. Deliberately varying fork prefix length to triangulate where the breakpoint sits is **out of scope** — note it as available follow-up if the result matters to M2.
- **The heartbeat break-even** — expressed as *how many beats an idle window can absorb before beating costs more than one cold write*, from measured (e) and (a). State the 1-hour-TTL cadence M2 should adopt, marked as inferred.
- **The collection-point observation** — `work/notes/26-07-25.child-collection-evidence.md` predicts a persistent superior is a collection point a turn ending cannot abandon. The charter asks whether it holds; record what the channel's behaviour across turns actually showed, even if the answer is "not exercised by this spike."

## Acceptance

Headlessly verifiable unless marked.

1. **The regime is verified by field read on every write cell** — the nested `usage.cache_creation` breakdown recorded per cell, showing which TTL each write claimed, with cell (d) cited as the behavioural cross-check that a 5m-tagged entry actually expires on schedule. No matrix cell is reported without its regime evidence.
2. **The ambient TTL is measured and the D1 recommendation written.** One unforced turn (no `FORCE_PROMPT_CACHING_5M`) reading the nested breakdown, plus the auth mode, recorded as its own line. If it confirms a 5-minute ambient default, the addendum carries the recommendation to amend charter D1 and move the production-TTL question to the retired spike-owned list — **as a recommendation only; amending a determination is the operator's.**
3. **All six cells captured** — a, b1, b2, c, d, e — each with the four flat `usage` fields, the nested `cache_creation` breakdown, wall-clock to first token, and a timestamp. The cold-fork and warm-fork cells carry **3 latency samples each**, reported as median plus spread; every other cell's latency carries its single-sample noise caveat. Raw stream logs for every cell retained under `tmp/spike/`.
3. **Ordering is auditable** — the log's timestamps show cell (a) preceded every warming touch and show each required idle gap actually elapsed.
4. **The heartbeat efficacy check ran** — beat-then-idle-then-real-turn, with the real turn's cache disposition recorded. Reported separately from cell (e)'s cost.
5. **The warm-fork corollary is ruled** — confirmed or refuted in one sentence, backed by b1's and b2's numbers, with b2's shared-prefix-versus-divergent-tail behaviour stated.
6. **The break-even is a number with its caveat attached** — beats-per-idle-window from measured (a) and (e), plus the recommended 1-hour-TTL cadence explicitly marked *inferred, not measured*.
7. **The evidence note carries the raw record** — a dated section in `work/notes/26-07-28.consult-token-cost-evidence.md` with the full per-cell table (numbers, not prose summary), the three verdicts, and the measured-versus-reasoned declaration; section-stamped per `work/README.md` § The callback grammar.
8. **The TTL finding lands as one connected item.** The reconnaissance finding about auth-dependent TTL, the ambient measurement that settled it, and the D1-amendment recommendation appear together in the evidence note — not scattered — so the gate reading and the operator's amendment decision happen against the same page.
8. **A recommendation is written, and it is only a recommendation** — the phase states go or no-go with its reasoning; it does not rule.
9. **The gate is ruled — operator-gated.** The operator reads the numbers and records go / no-go in `MILESTONE.md` § Acceptance. A no-go closes the mission unaccomplished (charter failure criterion 1) and the Executor stops rather than proceeding to M2.
10. **Quality gate green** — `bun .claude/bin/reference-gate check`, the tooling smoke test, and `/initialize` per `work/PROJECT.md` § All roles. Note that `work/notes/` is a gate exclusion and `work/milestones/` is a planning-artifact root (warns, never fails), so this phase should introduce no new failures; if it does, they are real.
11. **No product changed.** `git status` shows no modification under `.claude/`.

## Depends on

- **Phase 1 criterion 5's answer about where `usage` rides.** If per-turn `usage` is not emitted inside a multi-turn streaming process and only a terminal event carries it, the channel cells (c, d, e) must be restructured — most likely one process per measured turn, resumed rather than held open, which weakens cell (c)'s fidelity. Record the restructuring and its fidelity cost if it proves necessary; do not silently substitute.
- **The vehicle transcript from phase 1 is still present** and is the same one, so every cell shares a byte-exact prefix. A different vehicle between phases invalidates the matrix. Phase 1 records the vehicle's id and size; **re-check that recording against what is on disk at phase-2 start** — phase 1's own rationale acknowledges the local cache has a retention policy, so the risk this phase depends against is one the campaign has already named as live.
- **`--model fable` pinned on every spawn**, established in phase 1 § The spawn construction. Without it the cells compare different prefixes while appearing to measure one.
- **An operator-held quiet window** (operator ruling at the gate). Any other session running a consult or fork against the vehicle during an enforced idle gap silently destroys a cold measurement, leaving no trace in the harness's own log. The operator holds other Claude Code sessions off this repo for the duration of the matrix run; the Executor confirms the window is in place at the phase-1 gate before dispatching this phase, and the harness records the vehicle's mtime before each cold cell as corroborating evidence.
- **Phase 1's recorded last-touch timestamp** (phase 1 criterion 12) — the clock this phase's opening idle gap is measured from. That gap must clear the TTL **phase 1's touches used**, which was the *ambient* regime, not the forced one; the ambient measurement (criterion 2) is what licenses its length.
