# Phase 1 — floor: the declared-structure doctrine into canon

## Objective

Make declaredness + reconciliation a stated invariant of the workflow, and give every consuming project a schema to declare its structure against. The floor must be statable without a single project-shaped fact entering `.claude/` — that is the phase's hardest constraint and its acceptance test.

## Scope

**1. The doctrine — `work/README.md`.** A new section stating the invariant floor:

> Every consuming project declares its structure; the system owns reconciling actual against declared.

It names the **five audit axes** (from the binding proposal, verbatim in substance): root discipline · product/non-product separation · tracked-tree cleanliness · whole-canon reference integrity · work-tracking fidelity — each reading its project-specific content from the declaration. It states why four of the five are not universally *specifiable* (ecosystems force root files; any fixed allowlist the template ships is wrong somewhere) and that the meta-pattern inverted is the true maximal-minimum universal. It states the declaration's home (`work/PROJECT.md § Structure`) and adds a `PROJECT.md` row note in § Layout if the existing row does not already carry it.

The role-ladder row (Janitor) and the command-family row (`/clean`) are **phase 2's** — they describe artifacts that do not exist yet. Do not add them here.

**2. The schema — `.claude/BASELINE.md` bootstrap step 3.** Step 3 currently delegates `work/PROJECT.md` shape to "the template repository's own" file as exemplar. Expand it so the § Structure section is *specified*, not merely exemplified: the required subsections and the **template defaults** a project amends rather than invents —

- **root-file allowlist** — which files may sit at the organizational root, and the principle for amending it (ecosystem-forced files are declared, not fought).
- **product / non-product boundary** — what is the shipped artifact vs. what supports it.
- **package-root placement** — where packaging manifests live (this template's own rule — no packaging leaks to root — is an *instance* of the axis, stated generically as "the project declares its package roots").
- **archive convention** — where retired material goes.
- **sanctioned absences** (P5) — the list of paths that post-date the scaffold, created by convention as work begins, whose absence is not a gap. **This is the authoritative home for that list**, and it must be written as a list a mechanism can read.

Also extend `.claude/BASELINE.md` § Expectations (the `/initialize` audit targets) so the keystone-docs expectation names § Structure among the supplement questions a project must answer.

**3. The exemplar — `work/PROJECT.md § Structure`.** Author this repo's own declaration, filling all five subsections: the root-file allowlist for this tree, the `.claude/` = product boundary, `.claude/bin/package.json` as the sole sanctioned package root, `work/missions/<v>/` + `src/_archive/` as the archive conventions, and the sanctioned-absences list — **transcribed from the existing quality-gate recipe in § All roles item 1 (seven entries)**.

## The sequencing constraint — read before touching BASELINE

`.claude/BASELINE.md:49` names **three** post-scaffold paths. The gate recipe in `work/PROJECT.md` § All roles item 1 sanctions **seven**. This divergence is a *seeded subject* of the mission (charter D6, milestone P7): the dogfood pass in phase 4 must be able to detect it, and its reconciliation is that pass's work.

**Leave `.claude/BASELINE.md:49`'s three-path list exactly as it stands.** Do not edit it, do not add the four missing paths, do not rewrite it to point at the declaration. Authoring § Structure's sanctioned-absences list from the seven-entry gate recipe *preserves and sharpens* the divergence — which is the intent. Reconciling it here is a phase failure, not a helpful extra.

## Acceptance

1. `work/README.md` contains a section stating the declaredness + reconciliation invariant in the block-quoted form above and naming all five audit axes, each with a one-line statement of what it asks and that its content is read from the declaration.
2. `work/README.md` names `work/PROJECT.md § Structure` as the declaration's home.
3. `.claude/BASELINE.md` bootstrap step 3 specifies the § Structure schema: all five subsections named (root-file allowlist, product/non-product boundary, package-root placement, archive convention, sanctioned absences), each with a stated template default a project amends.
4. `.claude/BASELINE.md` § Expectations names § Structure among the keystone-doc supplement questions.
5. `work/PROJECT.md` has a `## Structure` section with all five subsections filled for this repo; its sanctioned-absences subsection lists exactly the seven entries currently named in § All roles quality-gate item 1.
6. **The seeded divergence is preserved**: `git diff` against the phase's base commit shows **no change** to `.claude/BASELINE.md`'s "Paths that post-date the scaffold" list.
7. **Genericity (local check)**: no project-shaped fact entered `.claude/` — verify by `grep -riE 'claude-workflow|orchestra|lasers|yarrow|/home/' .claude --include='*.md'` returning nothing beyond pre-existing generic placeholders, and by reading the step-3 defaults as a fresh consuming repo would.
8. The project quality gate passes (`work/PROJECT.md` § All roles items 1–3), including reference resolution over `.claude/`.

## Depends on
