# The dispatch ledger — give the `agentId` requirement a slot instead of a rule

> Proposal, 26-07-26, filed at operator direction from the M2 return-path close-out review. Seeding evidence: milestone M2 itself ([`work/milestones/2.return-path/MILESTONE.md`](../milestones/2.return-path/MILESTONE.md), closed accomplished 26-07-26) and its campaign log ([`work/todos/_done/26-07-26.execute-m2-return-path.md`](../todos/_done/26-07-26.execute-m2-return-path.md)). M2's own deferred list carries this as *"janitor axis-5 detection of recorded child `agentId`s at dispatch sites"*; this proposal argues detection is the second half of the remedy and the wrong half to start with.

## The problem (as evidenced, not hypothesized)

M2 landed the collection invariant in `work/README.md` and, with it, a requirement its own machinery depends on:

> **dispatchers record child `agentId`s in the work doc at dispatch time.**

Restated in `.claude/agents/manager/CLAUDE.md` § Turn discipline: *"record its `agentId` there at dispatch time — an agent cannot determine its own id, so any later continuation address must be injected from above."*

That requirement is **prose discipline**, landed by a milestone whose entire thesis is that prose discipline does not hold under pressure. The mission's own evidence for that thesis is amendment A3: a rule stated in the charter and breached **five times by well-briefed sessions** across two phases of M1. M2 exists because prose was the wrong instrument for a topology constraint; it then closed by adding a new prose constraint to the same class of reader.

**The empirical state, stated honestly in both directions.** The requirement *held* in its first outing — M2's phase-3 order records 13 child `agentId`s. But it held under conditions that do not generalize: the Executor's dispatch brief instructed the Manager to record them explicitly, and the Manager was building the very doctrine that demanded it. That is n=1 under direct supervision by the rule's own author. Meanwhile:

- **No work-order template, workflow step, or document convention carries an `agentId` field.** `grep -rn agentId` across `.claude/agents/manager/workflows/`, `.claude/agents/executor/workflows/` and `work/todos/README.md` returns **nothing**. The requirement is stated in canon and absent from every artifact a Manager actually fills in.
- The `## Progress` ledger — the one place a dispatch is naturally recorded as it happens — has no slot for it.

A rule stated where the reader is briefed, and absent from the artifact the reader writes, is exactly A3's shape.

## Why the failure is silent, which is what makes it worth a proposal

A missing `agentId` produces no error, no gate failure, and no wrong output. The order still closes; the verdict still issues.

What degrades is invisible: **send-then-read below the root** — canon's substitute for continuation — is *"a fresh dispatch briefed by the work doc, plus targeted reads of the prior agent's transcript or task output where held context genuinely matters (the recorded `agentId` is the address)."* With no recorded id there is no address, so restore-by-reading silently collapses into a plain fresh dispatch with no context restoration at all.

That is precisely the cost C12 warned about and that M2 argued it had avoided — *the fix for a context-leak defect costing context*. M2's close-out asserts the economy mostly survives. If the ids are not there, it does not survive, and **nothing anywhere reports that.** The system would look exactly as it does now.

## Why detection alone is the wrong instrument to start with

M2 deferred this as a **detection** item — an audit that finds dispatch sites lacking ids. Detection is worth having, but leading with it repeats the error:

- It is **after the fact**. It reports a context economy already lost on an order that already closed; nothing can be restored retroactively once the specialists are gone.
- It **adds a second rule to enforce the first**, leaving the original still carried by memory at the moment that matters.
- It inherits M2's own lesson in reverse. The milestone's finding was that the invariant works because *the shape of the idiom carries it* — the conversion rule's precondition is the invariant's own conclusion, so compliance is self-enforcing. The `agentId` requirement has no such shape. It should be given one.

## The remedy: a slot, not a rule

Make recording the **path of least resistance** rather than an extra step, by giving the dispatch record a home in the artifact that is already written at dispatch time.

The `## Progress` ledger is the natural host: it is appended as work happens, the Manager already writes to it per-step, and the Executor already reads it as the sanctioned mid-flight state (never the transcript). Adding the dispatch's identity to the line that already records the dispatch costs the writer nothing and removes the separate act of remembering.

**Candidates, priced.** Not pre-decided — this is the fork the proposal exists to surface:

| Option | What it does | Cost | Weakness |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **(a) Ledger slot** — the `## Progress` line for a dispatch carries its `agentId` | Recording rides an entry already being written | One convention line in `work/todos/README.md`; workflow steps mention it where they dispatch | Still prose, but prose describing a *slot that exists* rather than an act to remember |
| **(b) Detection** — audit dispatch sites for missing ids | Catches drift after the fact | A new check plus its jurisdiction question (below) | Reports a loss that can no longer be recovered |
| **(c) Order-close checklist item** | Manager confirms at close | Zero mechanism | Latest possible moment; the specialists are already gone |
| **(d) Blessing-time lens** | Architect checks the plan states it | Zero mechanism | Blessing precedes dispatch — it can require the slot but never observe the ids |

**Recommendation: (a) first, (b) after it has bedded in.** The slot is what changes behavior; detection then measures whether the slot worked, and measures something still worth knowing rather than something already lost. (c) and (d) are cheap enough to fold into (a) if wanted, but neither carries the load alone.

## The jurisdiction question adoption must settle first

M2 filed this as **janitor axis-5**, and that may be wrong. Axis 5 (work-tracking fidelity) **splits**: the `work/todos/` + `bugs/` slice — frontmatter, dispositions, archive routing, the prioritized ledger — *"delegates wholesale to `/triage`; the Janitor never re-audits it"*, while the Janitor's residual is milestone status rows, proposal `status:` vs placement, campaign-log filing, and footgun-index consistency.

Dispatch records inside a work order's `## Progress` ledger are **work-document content**, which reads as the `/triage` slice, not the janitor residual. So whichever detection lands, it lands in `/triage`'s workflow or nowhere — and if that is right, M2's deferral was filed against the wrong owner. This must be settled before any detection is built; it does not block the slot.

## What adoption would enact

1. A dispatch-record convention in `work/todos/README.md` — the `## Progress` ledger's dispatch entries carry the child's `agentId`.
2. The manager and executor workflow steps that dispatch reference the slot at the point of dispatch, replacing the free-floating requirement in `manager/CLAUDE.md` § Turn discipline with a pointer to where it is written.
3. `work/README.md` § The collection invariant's sentence rescoped from *"record them"* to *"record them in the ledger"* — one clause, naming the home.
4. The jurisdiction ruling above, and — if detection is wanted — a `/triage` check filed as its own typed order.

**Genericity holds**: all four are conventions about work-document shape, project-generic, carrying no mission facts and no version pins.

## What this proposal does not claim

It does not claim the requirement has been breached — it has not, once, under supervision. It claims the conditions that produced A3's five breaches are reproduced: a constraint stated where readers are briefed, absent from the artifact they write, with a silent failure mode and no gate. **The argument is structural, and the evidence for it is M2's own.**

It also does not claim urgency. Nothing is broken today, and the next campaign will run correctly if its Executor briefs the ids as M2's did. The reason to act before that is precisely that a supervised success is weak evidence, and the failure — when it comes — will not announce itself.
